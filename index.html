6. Three rules that win matchups

  • Reliable scoring is a floor. High scorers can produce points even without steals or blocks; defensive stats add valuable upside.
  • Usable games beat raw games. A pickup adds nothing if he must sit on your bench that day.
  • Protect long-term value. Don't lose a good player for one extra game. IL stashes can create roster flexibility, but they still count as acquisitions.
How to use it: First estimate the pickup's fantasy points per game, then enter the number of games he can actually start this week. Use the weekly difference to decide whether the move is worth the waiver and roster cost.
